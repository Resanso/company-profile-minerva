# FUTURE TECH — COMPANY PROFILE REDESIGN BRIEF

## 1. OBJECTIVE

Redesign company profile Future Tech so the company is positioned as an **industrial deep-tech company** with strong technology, a clear product platform, and credible real-world industrial validation.

The profile must answer four questions:

1. **Who is Future Tech?**
2. **What technology do we build?**
3. **What is MINERVA?**
4. **Has the technology actually been validated in the real world?**

### Core Positioning

# FUTURE TECH
## Industrial Intelligence for the Physical World

**MINERVA — Industrial Intelligence Platform**

### Core narrative

> Future Tech builds industrial intelligence systems that connect the physical world, industrial data, digital twins, and AI to enable better operational decisions.

# 4. DESIGN IMPLICATION OF THE AUDIENCE

The profile is **not primarily designed to impress other designers or technical engineers**.

It is designed to create confidence among **industrial decision makers**.

Therefore:

### First layer

**Business relevance**

> What does this solve?

### Second layer

**Technology credibility**

> How does it work?

### Third layer

**Evidence**

> Has it actually been done?

### Fourth layer

**Scalability**

> Can it work in other industrial environments?

The company profile should make the reader move through this thought process:

```text id="5f23o9"
"I understand the problem."
          ↓
"I understand the approach."
          ↓
"I understand MINERVA."
          ↓
"The technology seems credible."
          ↓
"They have actually built it."
          ↓
"They have validated it in industry."
          ↓
"This could potentially apply to my operation."
```

# 13. NON-NEGOTIABLES

1. **MINERVA must be the centerpiece of the technology narrative.**
2. **Ericsson Hackathon Winner 2025 must be clearly visible.**
3. **The aluminium production / smelting implementation must be clearly visible as real-world validation.**
4. Use actual documentation and photographs whenever possible.
5. Never fabricate quantitative impact.
6. Clearly distinguish PoC, pilot, implementation and deployment.
7. 5G is an enabler, not the main company category.
8. AI is a capability within industrial intelligence, not the entire company positioning.
9. Avoid positioning Future Tech as a generic system integrator.
10. FMCG and Ports & Logistics must be included in the industry/application section.
11. Technology ecosystem should focus on **Ericsson + other validated technology partners** only.
12. The profile must work for both **executive-level readers and technical stakeholders**, with business relevance first and technical depth second.
13. A reader should understand the company, product, and credibility **without a presenter explaining every page**.
14. The final impression should be:

> **“Future Tech has built the technology, received external recognition for it, and validated it in a real industrial environment.”**
